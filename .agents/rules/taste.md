# Taste & Aesthetic Standards

- **Warm editorial, not dashboard.** This site is set like a printed page: a display serif for headings, a companion sans for body copy, mono reserved for genuine metadata. It is deliberately *not* the Vercel/Linear dark-mode-SaaS look — that look, plus a single bright accent, is the most recognizable signature of a generated portfolio.
- **Vary the rhythm.** Sections must differ from their neighbours in vertical measure, container width and background. Five sections built on one `py-24 max-w-7xl` template with one shared heading component is the single strongest "this was generated" tell, regardless of how good the individual pieces are.
- **Typography is king.** High contrast for primary text, low contrast for secondary. Body copy is primary text at reading size — muted body copy is a template habit that makes the thing people came to read the least legible thing on the page.
- **Earn every box.** Prefer a hairline rule, a divider or plain type over a bordered card. If three sections would each be "an N-up grid of bordered cards with a hover-lift", at most one of them may be.
- **Depth is material, not decoration.** Elevation, overlap and surface texture are legitimate and encouraged — a flat page with only hairlines and text reads as under-designed, not restrained. Use the three shadow tokens in `app/globals.css` (`shadow-lift` for pressable things, `shadow-plate` for the project/portrait image plates, `shadow-float` for true overlays) and the matching `inset-shadow-lip/edge/well` tokens; assign them per element, not per component type — elevation is the exception, most of the page stays at level 0. `.plane-sheet` (About/Process/Contact/Footer) and `.plane-well` (image mounts) give the three surface tones a physical role instead of three flat fills. Prefer overlap (an image bleeding past its column, a folio numeral sitting behind the text that occludes it) before reaching for a new shadow — it gives depth for free. What stays banned is the specific vocabulary that signals "generated": blurred accent-colored orbs, glassmorphism, glow shadows, faux window chrome (macOS traffic lights), grid backdrops, and text-shadow. A shadow is decoration, not material, if it fails any of: no accent hue at any opacity; `offset-x = 0` and `blur >= 2x|offset-y|`; `spread <= 0`, and `<= -0.45x blur` past 8px of blur; `blur <= 64px`; composited result darker than the surface it lands on.
- **No fake telemetry.** No uptime percentages, proficiency bars, count-up counters, pulsing "available" dots, or vanity stats for a person. If a number is on the page it must be verifiable.
- **Icons stand for things, not ideas.** Brand marks for real technologies are fine. An icon picked to represent an abstract noun ("Adaptability" → a shuffle glyph) is filler — and if it is keyed by array index it is also a latent i18n bug.
- **No default colours.** Never pure red or pure blue. Tailored, slightly desaturated, harmonic shades only. Check contrast and record it; never fix a contrast failure by adding an `/opacity` modifier to text.
- **Generous, uneven whitespace.** Interfaces should breathe, but symmetric padding everywhere reads as a template. Let some things sit tight and others sit wide.
- **Zero visual noise.** Remove anything that does not serve a direct purpose. Less is more.
